# My Portfolio

This is my personal portfolio website. I made it to share my experience in IT, cybersecurity, and networking, along with the projects I’m working on and what I’m learning.

I’m Alyk Lippincott, an Information Technology Engineer at Ahresty Wilmington Corporation and a cybersecurity and networking student at the University of Cincinnati. I wanted a place where people could get to know me and see my work beyond a résumé.

[View my portfolio](https://lippinay.github.io/Portfolio.github.io/)

## About the site

The site includes my work experience, technical skills, education, learning credentials, résumé, and contact information. I kept the design simple with a dark theme, green accents, and a video background on the home page. The layout also adjusts for phones and smaller screens.

The projects section highlights my Proxmox Home Lab, a NIST-based risk assessment and incident response project, a Bash CSV filtering tool, and ZombieGuard, the senior design project I’m developing with my team. Each project has a short overview with more details available to expand.

## How it’s built

I used HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

I used my prior knowledge, YouTube tutorials, and some AI assistance to help with parts of the design, code, and wording.

- `index.html` contains the page content and layout.
- `style.css` handles the design and responsive styling.
- `script.js` handles the background video controls and navigation highlighting.
