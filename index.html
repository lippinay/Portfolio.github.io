SENIOR DESIGN / TEAM PROJECT IN PROGRESS

ZombieGuard

A team senior design project to discover forgotten accounts and help users understand potential breach exposure.

My role: Full-stack Developer / Project Floater.

Gmail API XposedOrNot Account discovery Privacy-conscious design
Read project details

Developing a senior design project with a team at the University of Cincinnati to help users discover forgotten online accounts and understand their potential breach exposure.

The planned workflow uses explicitly authorized Gmail metadata—sender, subject, and date—to identify account-related emails, then checks the user’s email address against known breach data through XposedOrNot. A centralized dashboard will prioritize accounts for review and recommend actions such as password updates, multi-factor authentication, or account removal.

Designed for metadata-only access and session-based data handling. Cleanup actions remain under the user’s control. Development and demonstrations are planned for team-approved test accounts.
